## Introduction   

WhatsApp **Status** lets you share photos, videos, GIFs, text, and voice updates with your contacts. The update disappears automatically after 24 hours.

You can add music to supported status updates. WhatsApp supports videos up to 90 seconds long in 3GP and MPEG-4 formats.

Status privacy settings help you control who see’s your status.  You can find status updates in the **Updates** tab. 

Viewers can like your status or reply to it in a chat. Only you can see who likes your status.

The contacts that you tag in your photo and video status can reshare your content. This is done without revealing your name, profile information, or phone number. 

WhatsApp lets you check who views your status. Read receipts must be enabled for you and your contacts to see status views.

>**Note:** 

> WhatsApp does not allow disabling the Status feature completely.

> High definition media is not supported.  

> It is advised to upload high quality images for better experience. 

## Benefits

WhatsApp Status offers a convenient way to share what’s on your mind with multiple people in your contact list instantly. 

Following are the key benefits: 

* **Instant Update:** WhatsApp Status allows you to share an update with your contacts instantly. It also reduces the overall real-time communication overhead and saves time. 

* **Automatic Status Expiry:** Status posted on WhatsApp is deleted automatically in 24 hours, eliminating the need for manual intervention. 

* **Efficient Communication:** Keeps contacts updated without starting a separate conversation. The contacts can view the update as per their convenience within 24 hours of posting. 

## Pricing   

Status is a free of cost feature available for android and iOS devices. There is no separate charge applicable to access or use the status feature. It is included in the standard experience. For WhatsApp business also there are no separate charges levied to use the status update feature. 

## Updating Status on WhatsApp   

Update what’s on your mind using photos, text, videos, GIF’s and music with your contacts at once via the **Update** feature available on Android and iOS. 

This section explains how to use the different status options available. 

Learn how to use the following:

* Share a text status.
* Share a photo or video status.
* Share AI Images as status.
* Share a voice status.

### Updating AI Image as Status

WhatsApp’s status update comes with an AI Image generator that lets you create an image using text prompts and post it is as a status. Built on by Meta, this feature comes with a set of templates that can be used as is or with little modification to the pre-loaded prompt. 

**Prerequisites**

* Active WhatsApp Application 
* Active Internet 
* Availability of Meta AI 

**Add AI Image as Status Update**

To add AI image as your WhatsApp status update, follow these steps:

1. Open **WhatsApp** on your device.
1. On the left bottom corner Click **Updates**.

4.  On the top pane Swipe right.
5.  Select **AI Images** option on the top frame.
6.  On the next screen select one of the following:  

* **Create Image:**
  * Enter a new prompt.
  * Select a template and edit the preloaded text prompt.

* **Edit Photo:**

8.  (Optional): Restyle Image using any of the following:

* **Featured:** Provides ready-to-use AI image ideas based on popular or highlighted themes.

**Result:** An image built or enhanced using AI is updated and available for views and reaction from your chosen contacts, for 24 hours. 

### Updating Photo or Video as Status   

To share a photo or video as your WhatsApp Status, follow these steps:

1.  Open **WhatsApp** on your device.

2.  At the bottom of the screen tap **Updates**.

3.  Tap **Add Status**.

4.  Select a photo or video from your gallery, or tap the **Camera** option to capture new media.

5.  Edit the photo or video using the available options:

* Add a caption.
* Add text, stickers, or emojis.
* Draw on the media or crop the photo.
* Add music which you can select from:
  * Suggested 
  * Mood
  * Genre
  * Starred

6.  (Optional) Tap the Status audience option to choose who can view your update.

7.  (Optional) For a video, drag the slider to trim it.

8.  Tap Share or the Send icon to publish your status.

**Result:** The photo or video appears as your WhatsApp Status and remains available for 24 hours.
